# ADR-NNNN: <short title>

> **TL;DR:** <decision/ruling · load-bearing why · Status + date — telegraph, ≤ ~50 words, standalone (readers stop here)>

- **Status**: Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by ADR-MMMM | Withdrawn | Deprecated

  *`Withdrawn` — never Accepted; the premise was refuted (usually by measurement) or the decision was abandoned before ratification. Keep the body as the record of **why**, so the idea is not rebuilt by reflex. `Superseded` is different and must not be repurposed for this: it marks an **Accepted** decision that a later ADR replaced.*
- **Date**: YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders**: <who>

## Context

What is the situation? What forces are in play (technical, user-facing, the measured evidence)? Cite `docs/notes/` where a claim rests on something we measured.

## Decision

What did we decide? Stated as a clear, present-tense sentence.

## Consequences

What becomes true as a result? Both positive and negative. What new constraints or maintenance costs are we accepting? Call out explicitly anything that ties us to a private Apple format or behaviour, since those change without notice.

## Alternatives considered

For each alternative: what was it, and why not? Two or three lines each. The point is to record the *reasoning*, not exhaustively catalogue every option.
